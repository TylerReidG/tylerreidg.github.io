The trek · self-guided

Salkantay to Machu Picchu

About 71 km over four walking days. We sleep in simple lodges with dinner and breakfast included.

Elevations in metres, approximate. Swipe the chart sideways to see it all.

Packing

What to bring

Ranges are a guide, so pick what suits you. Tick things off as you pack; ticks are saved on your own phone or computer. Missing something? Say so in the group chat and we'll see who has extras.

Good to know

Before you go

Weather

15–20°C by day and 5–7°C at night, with rain most days. On the trek, plan for about 0°C, wet and maybe snow on the coldest night.

Altitude

Cusco sits at 3,400 m. Take the first two days slowly: lots of water, short walks, go easy on drinks.

Machu Picchu

Entry is for a timed slot, about two hours inside, with a licensed guide. Bring your passport.

Warm layers

Alpaca sweaters, toques and gloves are cheap in Cusco if you want to pick some up there.
